Vega Launcher Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Who provides Vega

Vega Launcher is provided by Albert J. Vega under the developer name The Aggie Project. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you send to the developer, contact theaggieproject.support@gmail.com. This policy covers Vega Launcher, including its Google Play and separately distributed GitHub/testing builds. Features and permissions differ between distribution channels.

Launcher data on your device

Vega reads installed-app identities, names and icons to display and search your apps, organize folders and groups, launch apps, and identify apps referenced by a restored backup. It stores your layout, preferences, gestures, appearance presets, imported fonts, chosen images and recovery records in app storage. Launcher interactions and, if you grant Android usage access, app-usage statistics can be used locally to rank app suggestions.

Optional contact search reads contact information after you enable the feature and grant Contacts permission. Optional notification access supports badges and notification-related launcher features. Optional file/audio search reads the categories Android allows and you enable. These features do not by themselves send your full contacts, notification contents, files or installed-app inventory to a Vega-operated server. Online suggestions and your explicit exports are separate paths described below.

The Google Play build uses system-selected images for folder art and wallpaper backups rather than broad photo/video or all-files permissions. GitHub builds can offer broader file/media access when Android permits it and you grant it. Work profiles and Private Space remain subject to Android visibility and lock restrictions.

Online search and external links

Web suggestions are enabled by default in the current release. When enabled, typing a nonblank search query can send that text to your selected suggestion provider: Google, DuckDuckGo, Startpage (including its EU endpoint), or Kagi. A provider receives the query and normal connection information such as your IP address and HTTP request metadata. Disable web suggestions in Search bar settings if you do not want these requests. Text entered while searching for a local app or contact can also be sent as a query when online suggestions are enabled; avoid typing sensitive information in that mode.

Opening a web result, app-store listing, support link or external search action passes the selected query, package name or link to the browser or app you choose. Those providers handle your interactions under their own policies. Vega does not control their logs, accounts, retention or deletion procedures.

Fonts, widgets and integrations

Bundled fonts and imported font files can be used locally. Choosing a downloadable Google font requests its family and style through the Google Play services font provider; that provider may download it and process connection information under Google policies.

Widgets, feed bridges, Smartspacer and other enabled integrations are provided by separate apps or services. They may process information under their own policies. If you authorize Tasker integration, Vega exchanges the configured task names, launcher-event information and saved-layout identifiers with Tasker to carry out your chosen actions. Tasker and configured tasks can perform their own data transfers; Vega does not control those tasks.

The GitHub release channel can contact GitHub to check for and download Vega updates, sending normal network request information. The Play package does not use Vega’s APK self-update path. Opening external project or contributor resources can also contact their hosts.

Accessibility access

Accessibility access is optional and is used for configured system actions such as locking the screen, showing recent apps, opening notifications or Quick Settings, including authorized deterministic Tasker actions. Vega is not primarily a disability-assistance tool.

The current Vega accessibility service requests no accessibility event types and has an empty accessibility-event callback. It does not request screen-content retrieval or use the service to read text you type, inspect other apps’ screen contents, or send accessibility events to the developer. Enabling it still grants a sensitive Android service permission. You can decline it or turn it off in Android Accessibility settings; features that require it will be unavailable.

Diagnostics and support

Vega can generate local crash or diagnostic reports. Reports can include the app version, build/OS and device-model details, diagnostic state, error messages and stack traces. Error text can contain app, configuration or file references, so review a report before sharing it.

Reports are not automatically uploaded by Vega’s crash handler. If you choose Upload for a report, Vega sends its text to the third-party paste service katb.in over HTTPS and receives a report link. Treat uploaded report links as potentially public. Sharing a report or copying error details sends or copies it only after your action to the destination you choose. Third-party hosting and receiving apps control their own retention; deleting a local report does not delete an uploaded copy.

When you email support, the developer receives the message, your sender address and attachments you include. Support information is used to respond, investigate problems and maintain the app, and retained as needed for those purposes or applicable legal requirements. You can request deletion of information held by the developer by emailing support. Vega cannot promise deletion of records held independently by your selected providers.

Backups, presets and sharing

Manual backups can include launcher settings/layout, selected wallpaper images, and optional app names, icon thumbnails and Play Store restore links. Scheduled backups export launcher layout/settings and registered launcher assets to your chosen folder. They do not include installed APK files or other apps’ private data. Imported fonts, folder art and other registered assets can be included in layout backups.

Documents you save or share may leave the device if you select a cloud storage or receiving app. Your chosen provider determines storage, account access, security and retention. Vega does not operate a backup cloud or require a Vega account. Backup documents can contain sensitive app-inventory and layout information; keep them in a destination you trust. Sharing an appearance preset exports its saved style and any included assets, not a complete copy of your launcher backup.

Android’s own backup or device-transfer services may also copy supported app data according to your system settings and the app’s backup rules. That is separate from Vega’s manual and scheduled backup folders.

Retention, deletion and controls

Local preferences, layouts and imported assets remain until you change/remove them or clear the app’s data. Temporary caches and locally generated diagnostic files may be cleaned by the app or Android. Clearing data or uninstalling normally removes app-private data from that installation, but does not remove exported documents, shared/uploaded copies, system backups or provider-held data.

Vega does not provide an account-registration system, so there is no Vega account to delete. You can disable online suggestions, revoke optional permissions and integrations in Android or Vega settings, remove local imported items, and manage backup/preset/report documents in the storage or receiving app. Restoring a backup can restore saved settings, but does not grant Android permissions by itself.

Security and third parties

Vega’s built-in suggestion, diagnostic-upload and GitHub update endpoints use HTTPS. Local app-private storage is protected by Android’s application sandbox; Vega does not add password encryption to exported backup archives. Protect exported files and your device accordingly. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Providers involved may include Google (search/fonts/system services), DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Kagi, GitHub, katb.in, your document/email/browser apps, Tasker and enabled widget/feed integrations. Their policies and controls apply to their processing. Vega does not sell your personal data or use the installed-app inventory to target advertisements.

Changes and contact

This policy describes the current implementation and may be revised when features or data handling change. Review the effective date when installing updates. Privacy requests and questions: theaggieproject.support@gmail.com.
